# MilkCrate for Mac (Apple Silicon) — closed beta

1. Unzip this file.
2. Drag **MilkCrate** to Applications or Desktop.
3. Open the app. If macOS says it is from an unidentified developer:
   right-click → **Open**, or System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Open Anyway**.
4. Create an account (name + email). Required for this beta.
5. Drop a folder of tracks. After your first export, a feedback prompt appears.
   You can also use **Report a problem** anytime.
6. Optional: **Share corrections**, **Hot cues**, **Export**, **Send back to USB**.

This is a test build. It may mis-tag tracks or crash. Do not post the zip publicly.

Needs macOS 13+ on Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4).
